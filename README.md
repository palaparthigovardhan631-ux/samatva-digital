# Samatva Digital (समत्व डिजिटल | సమత్వ డిజిటల్ | சமத்துவ டிஜிட்டல்)
### National Civic & Welfare Integrity Platform • Public Goods Ecosystem

> **Motto:** *Objective Needs-Based Welfare • Zero Middlemen • Transparent Governance*

---

## 🌟 Executive Summary

**Samatva Digital** is an advanced, responsive civic-tech and welfare integrity portal designed to solve critical systemic challenges in public service delivery:
1. **Bridging Citizen-Government Miscommunication:** Multilingual AI advisors in English, Hindi, Telugu, and Tamil.
2. **Replacing Identity Bias with Needs-Based Quotas:** 100% objective vulnerability rule engine prioritizing actual economic distress, asset deprivation, and first-generation learner status.
3. **Eliminating Intermediary Leakages:** Real-time Graph AI fund routing monitoring from Central Treasury down to Aadhaar-linked citizen vaults.
4. **Stopping Bureaucratic Extortion:** 100% anonymous, client-side encrypted whistleblower portal with direct state escalation bypassing corrupt local panchayats.
5. **Eradicating Arbitrary Rejections:** DigiLocker API integration paired with pre-submission AI diagnostic scanning to catch document defects prior to official filing.
6. **Crowdsourced Ground Reality Verification:** Citizen-led audits on sanctioned public schools, clinics, and clean water plants, backed by an automated 7-day statutory reconsideration appeal bot.

## 🏛️ Core Architectural Modules

### 1. Universal Needs-Based Eligibility & Reservation Engine (Module 1)
- **Conversational Multilingual AI:** Citizens can type or prompt questions in regional colloquial languages (Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, English).
- **Interactive Hardship Parameters:**
  - Annual Household Income slider (₹50k - ₹10L+)
  - Primary Livelihood classification (Daily wage, landless agri-labor, artisan, street vendor, gig driver)
  - Landholding & asset deprivation index
  - Objective hardship modifiers (+8 pts First-Gen College Learner, +8 pts Single Parent/Orphan, +10 pts PwD, +6 pts Rural Schooling, +6 pts Chronic Medical Debt)
- **Zero-Bias Seal:** Explicitly excludes traditional caste and social identity markers (0% weighting applied) in favor of verifiable economic hardship.
- **Explainable AI Breakdown:** Mathematical scorecard detailing exact points awarded, Samatva Eligibility Tier (Tiers 1 through 4), and unlocks full tuition waivers, living stipends, and free digital hardware.

### 2. Anti-Leakage & Direct Welfare Fund Tracker (Module 2)
- **Macro-Integrity Metrics:**
  - Total Sanctioned Welfare (₹48,420 Cr)
  - Grassroots DBT Disbursed (₹46,890 Cr - 96.84%)
  - Intermediary Leakage Prevented (₹1,530 Cr)
  - National Integrity Index Score (94.2 / 100)
- **District-wise Audit Table:** Searchable by district name, state, and status tags (`Optimal / Clear`, `Bottleneck Flagged`, `Audit Triggered`, `High Discrepancy`).
- **Graph AI Flow Topology:** Real-time visualization of Central Treasury $\to$ State Nodal Account $\to$ District Clearing Gateway $\to$ Aadhaar-linked Citizen Vault.
- **Oversight Interventions:** Ability for administrators to freeze suspicious intermediate escrows or trigger direct DBT overrides.

### 3. Anti-Corruption & Anonymous Grievance Portal (Module 3)
- **100% Anonymous Whistleblower Protection:** Client-side AES-256 encryption, zero IP logging, and automated device EXIF metadata stripping.
- **Direct State Escalation:** Circumvents local block panchayats to route tickets directly to the State Anti-Corruption Bureau (ACB) and Lokayukta.
- **Cryptographic Tracking Token:** Generates tamper-proof ticket IDs (e.g. `#SD-8921-X`) with QR simulation.
- **Immutable Audit Trail:** Real-time visibility into investigation assignments, evidence spectrograph verification, and officer disciplinary restitution.

### 4. Digital Vault & Pre-Submission Verifier (Module 4)
- **DigiLocker Integration:** Syncs official income certificates, 12th marksheets, domicile proofs, NFSA ration cards, and NPCI bank seeding status.
- **Automated AI Pre-Checker:** Scans for certificate expiration (e.g., 1-year validity for income certificates), cryptographic QR checksums, and cross-document phonetic name matching.
- **Interactive Error Simulation:** Toggle on/off to experience how the pre-checker catches expired certificates and provides 1-click renewal recommendations.
- **Direct Pipeline Submission:** Generates an official `SAMATVA-APP` token with zero clerical intervention.

### 5. Crowdsourced Ground Reality Audit & Legal Rights (Module 5)
- **Ground Reality Tracker:** Citizen ratings, geo-tagged coordinates, and photo reviews on sanctioned public infrastructure (Primary School Solar Labs, PHC Dialysis Wings, Village RO Water Plants, Ghost Facilities).
- **Constitutional Rights Guide:** Non-legalese explanations of Article 14, 15, 21, the Right to Public Services Act (₹250/day penalty on delinquent clerks), and Prevention of Corruption Act (POCA).
- **The 7-Day Statutory Appeal Bot:** Form that automatically generates a formal legal notice of appeal under Section 5 of the Public Services Guarantee Act, forcing local administrations to issue written justification within 7 days.

- **Celebration Effects:** `canvas-confetti`

- **Languages Supported:** English, Hindi (हिंदी), Telugu (తెలుగు), Tamil (தமிழ்)
- **Dual Role Matrix:** Citizen Portal vs. Oversight Administration Mode

## 🚀 Running the Project Locally

```bash
# Navigate to project directory
cd samatva-digital

# Install dependencies (if not already installed)
npm install

# Start Vite development server
npm run dev -- --port 5174

# Build for production
npm run build
```

Open **`http://localhost:5174/`** in any modern web browser.
